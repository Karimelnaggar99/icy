## What makes these questions work (for the app flow)
1. Start light, escalate gradually. Aron's finding: back-and-forth disclosure that increases slowly builds closeness; too much too fast backfires. WNRS uses the same ladder (perception, connection, reflection). Maps to ICY levels 1-3.
2. Reciprocity: both people answer. Aron's procedure and Science of People both say to answer your own question; the app should make "you answer too / turn passes" explicit.
3. Follow-ups beat new questions. A Harvard study (cited by Science of People) found follow-up questions drive likability. Add a "tell me more" nudge after each answer.
4. Break the rhythm. WNRS wild cards and "silly/laughter" sections give a pause from heavy rounds; insert a fun card every 3-4 know cards.
5. Open, story-inviting, positive framing ("best", "favourite", "what were you like as a kid") beats yes/no and beats stock probes ("are you close to your family?"). Travel, tastes, childhood and "typical day" are singled out repeatedly.
6. Soften sensitive items (regret, crying, struggles); keep them out or late. Avoid the interrogation feel: let either person skip a card.
